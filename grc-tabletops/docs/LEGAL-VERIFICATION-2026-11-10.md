## Still unverified

- **Lembaga PDP establishment.** A search on `peraturan.go.id` for "lembaga
  pelindungan data pribadi" returned UU 27/2022 but no establishment instrument.
  This does not prove the institution does not exist; it means no official
  instrument was found in that search. Tabletop participants must refer to
  "lembaga" under the law and state a verification plan rather than naming an
  agency or address.
- **Implementing regulations of UU PDP.** Not found in the same official search.
  Several articles require a Government Regulation; do not assert that one exists.
- **Sectoral higher-education data rules.** Not verified; do not cite.
- **MK decision 151/PUU-XXII/2024.** Only a summary page was seen; the decision
  text was not reviewed.

## Implication for the tabletop

- A defensible answer may say: "PP 71/2019 is still in force; the PDP authority
  and implementing regulations must be verified before relying on a specific
  channel."
- Do not award points for naming an unverified agency, address, or regulation.
- Award points for distinguishing what is verified from what is assumed, and for
  stating a verification step.

## Maintenance

Re-check the two `peraturan.go.id` pages and the official JDIH search before each
cohort. Update this note and the mentor key together.
